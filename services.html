OUR SERVICES

ADOPTION PROCESS OVERVIEW

Adopting a pet gives an animal in need the opportunity to become part of a loving family. Our adoption process helps connect animals with homes that are a good fit for their needs.

ADOPTION PROCESS

  1. Explore available pets and find an animal that may be a good fit.
  2. Submit an adoption inquiry.
  3. Meet with the animal and a member of the rescue team.
  4. Complete the adoption process and welcome your new pet home.

FOSTER PROGRAM OVERVIEW

Foster families provide temporary, safe, and caring homes for animals while they wait for their permanent families. Fostering helps animals receive individual attention and gives the rescue valuable information about each animal's personality and needs.

WHAT FOSTER FAMILIES PROVIDE

  • A safe and welcoming temporary home
  • Daily care, attention, and socialization
  • Updates about the animal's personality and behavior
  • Support in preparing the animal for adoption

VOLUNTEER OPPORTUNITIES

Volunteers play an important role in helping Twin Cities Animal Rescue care for animals and connect with the community. There are several ways volunteers can contribute their time and skills.

ANIMAL CARE

Help provide animals with attention, socialization, and basic care while they wait for their permanent homes.

COMMUNITY EVENTS

Assist at adoption events and community outreach activities that introduce people to the rescue and its animals.

ADOPTION SUPPORT

Help potential adopters learn more about available animals and the adoption process.
